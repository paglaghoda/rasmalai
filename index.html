Everything i make, put my mind on is an art to me. I maybe an engineer, a product manager, a developer in my mind - but in my heart there's only i thing i like and only i thing i aim to become An Artist.

Why Arstist? An Artist considers every art of him as a reflection of Who he is and What he stands for in this world - his values and that is deeply resonating to me. Anything i make or do has to be perfect, not because someone else would see it, but because it reflects what i stand for and how i think - that is important to me.

An art doesn't just exist - it conveys emotions and trauma and that is beautiful. The beauty of an art lies in the traumas of the man holding the brush making it - and that shows he has had life shape him. Similarly, i consider every project of me as me.
